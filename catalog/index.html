About Bridging Craft

A connection in the making.

After 20 years in marketing in the consumer packaged goods industry, Catalina Rivera is developing her path into art, craft, and interiors. Bridging Craft is a work in progress: a place to deepen an understanding of makers, materials, and cultural value, and explore how craft can become part of thoughtfully collected lives and spaces.
